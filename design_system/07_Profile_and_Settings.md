# ThriveSpace Profile & Settings Experience
*Professional, Minimal, Calming Personal Wellness Management*

## 👤 Profile Overview

The Profile section serves as both a personal wellness dashboard and account management hub. It reflects the user's journey, achievements, and preferences while providing access to all app settings, privacy controls, and account features. The design emphasizes personal reflection over social comparison.

### Profile Principles
- **Personal Reflection**: Focus on individual growth, not social metrics
- **Privacy Control**: Granular control over personal data sharing
- **Wellness Journey**: Visual representation of personal progress
- **Professional Growth**: Recognition of learning and achievements
- **Mindful Customization**: Thoughtful personalization options
